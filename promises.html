CHAPTER 03 · THE FOREVER

Not just for tonight.
For a lifetime.

Love is in what we choose, again and again.
Here are a few things I want to keep choosing for you.

01

To be your safe place.

To listen with patience, hold you with kindness, and make room for every version of you. You never have to have it all together with me.

♡
02

To choose the little things.

The hand held without asking. The message in the middle of a busy day. The time set aside just for us. I want my love to be something you can feel.

♡
03

To stand beside your dreams.

Your dreams matter to me. I want to cheer the loudest for your wins and help you find your courage when the next step feels hard.

♡
04

To keep finding our laughter.

To be a little silly, to make room for joy, and to remember that being with my favourite person is already a reason to smile.

♡
05

To keep choosing us.

Through changing seasons and ordinary days. To talk things through, say sorry when I should, and keep building a love that feels like home.
