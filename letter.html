A LITTLE LOVE NOTE ♡

My dearest Shilpa,

I wanted to give you something you could come back to. On a beautiful day, on a difficult day, or on an ordinary afternoon when you just need to know: you are so deeply loved.

When I look at our photographs, I don’t just see our wedding. I see the person I get to build a life with. The person I want beside me for the big adventures, the silly conversations, and the quiet evenings when we don’t need to say very much at all.

On Karwa Chauth, I’m thinking of your love, your care, and everything you give so generously. And I want you to know that my wish is for you, too: your happiness, your health, your dreams, and a life where you always feel cherished.

You are not just part of my life.
You are what makes it feel like home.

I may not always find the perfect words. But I want to keep finding the little ways to say them: listening properly, showing up, making you laugh, holding your hand, and choosing us even on the days that ask a little more of us.

Always on your team. Always in your corner.
